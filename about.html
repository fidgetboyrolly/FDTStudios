power ups

fire

the fire power up make it so you burst into flame when you roll, igniting everything. this power-up will be the most common

electric

the lectric power-up is a version of the fire power up that is temporary but grants imunity to all enemies and the ability to almost fly

these next few power ups may not be added to the full game but there is a chance a few of them will

mirror

the mirror power upallows you to walk though reflective walls and you can blind enemies if you roll to close to them, they can still kill you though

light

the light power up allows you to stun every enemy on screen and you can reflec off mirrors to gain jump height or help speedrun a level

water

water will boost your swim speed and you can roll into a ball of water, this power up won't let you walk on water

gecko

this power up allows you to hide from enemies but you can't move

gravity
