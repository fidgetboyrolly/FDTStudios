Blockheads started as three lego bricks, a white 2x2 tile, a blue 2x2 brick, and a pair of white legs, this was the start of wattson

Fast forward a few years and you find my first relevent scratch game; intergalactic bread fighting this project has been repaired since then, rendering the original as lost media, but it was up for a few years

The next major step in the history of development was an old project called realistic company simulator this project was the first project of mine that used penguinmod instead of scratch, that game never was realesed, the version attatch to this website is a modified version that has less bugs.

The first (and only) project that was posted on the offical penguinmod website is intergalactic bread fighting mobile this game probably has the least bugs of any game I have ever made, this was not at all a succses, it was actually quite ignored, not long after though I started to work on a sequle, a new version of the game my be published based on the code for the sequle, but I will never realese the sequle as sequle.

Now you may not quite get why almost 3 years have passed since blockheads has made any progress, but I will get to that soon

The next major project I made was StrudleOS, this project was a much bigger project, this project's goal was to create an bootable ISO images that you could boot your PC off of, the project stoped when I tried to achiive the main goal of the project, how it was supossed to work was a penguinmod project would be packaged as a electron app, the app would then be "kiosked" to a OS like Debain (linux) then if I use the right tools I could then package the OS as an ISO, the problem came at the kiosking step, no matter what I tried I could not get it to work, when the intrest for the project vanished, so did my determination to work on it

During the development of StrudleOS I had to make a demo for an event, the demo was StrudleOS running on a Raspbery Pi the build of StrudleOS had a few apps, onw of them was blockheads: horrific demo which as you can tell is not my favorite project, but it was the first blockheads project that I have ever made. Even though the StrudleOS demo interested a few people the only thing that was interesting was the Blockheads demo, not long after that I ended development on StrudleOS

Then I made the fist blockheads game, blockheads: Box2D test this game gave me many reasons to restart from scratch, 1. the physics engine was pure jank 2. the graphics looked like a over saturated lime had died on it and even wattson's controls were annoying, but it was something

Around this time the All the Mods Game Jam had started, a game jam that allowed users of any sratch mod to upload a game for a chance to win a $100 gift card, I join a team that, kicked me out not long after because I made to many jokes, but after a few day i decided i might still be abled to so it, so I uplaoded un-click'et a game where you play as a wizard who was known for... "un-anything-ing" and you were summoned to a small town where a guy named "Dave" accidentally leaned on the "nuke us pls" button, which told a nearby city that the city you were in wanted to be nuked (if I ever remaster ths game I will add a reson behind the button), your job was to un-click the button, but they took your stick (not a magic stick just a normal stick) at the door so the best you could do was click it again, so he dropped you into a pit (this is a REALLY bit pit by the way) in which you have to survive and get out of, am I explaining all my other games more that I should?

Finnaly, after the game jam stuff was over I started FDT studios, not a leagal body/company or anything, just me and a friend, and at that time I started the current version of blockheads which is my longest on going project and personal favorite
